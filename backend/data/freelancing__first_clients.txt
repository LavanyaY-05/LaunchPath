Getting Your First Freelance Clients

Platform strategy: New profiles get poor visibility on hourly contracts — starting with 2-3 small, fixed-price projects to build reviews works better.

Portfolio approach: A focused set of 4-6 strong samples outperforms a large number of mediocre ones.

Direct outreach: For local or niche work, direct outreach to businesses that clearly need the service is an underused channel.

Common mistake: Underpricing early work, making it hard to raise rates later.
