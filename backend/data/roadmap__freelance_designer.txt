Roadmap: Freelance Graphic/UI Designer

Skill order: Design fundamentals -> one core tool (Figma) -> basic UX principles -> a focused portfolio of 5-6 strong pieces -> client presentation skills.

Platform path: Behance/Dribbble for portfolio visibility, general freelance marketplaces for first paid work. Direct outreach to small businesses is often an underused early-client source.

Common early mistake: Pricing purely by hours instead of value delivered.
