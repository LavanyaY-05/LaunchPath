What Makes a Strong Portfolio (Any Freelance Field)

Format: A personal website, PDF one-pager, Notion page, or platform profile — format matters far less than content and clarity.

Structure that works: 4-6 focused pieces, each with: what the problem was, what you did, and the outcome.

Role-specific application: For designers, include process artifacts (wireframes, iterations, before/after comparisons), not just final polished screens.

Common mistake: Showing volume over relevance — 3 highly relevant pieces outperform 15 loosely related ones.
