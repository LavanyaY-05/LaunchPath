Pricing Yourself as a Freelancer

Hourly vs fixed: Fixed-price quotes protect your effective hourly rate for defined-scope work; hourly billing works better for open-ended work.

Value-based thinking: Pricing based on value delivered rather than hours spent tends to result in fairer, higher pricing.

Raising rates: Gradual increases with new clients is the more common, lower-friction path than renegotiating with existing ones.

Common mistake: Quoting a price without clearly defining scope boundaries first, leading to scope creep.
