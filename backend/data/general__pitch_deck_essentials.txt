What a Strong Early-Stage Pitch Deck Should Contain

Core sections investors expect: Problem, Solution, Market size, Product, Traction (even early signals), Business model, Competition/differentiation, Team, The Ask.

Common gaps in early decks: Missing a Traction section, missing a concrete funding ask, listing competitors without a clear differentiation statement.

Length and format: 10-15 slides is a common early-stage norm.
