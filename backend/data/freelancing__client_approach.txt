How to Approach and Communicate with Freelance Clients

First message: Lead with a specific observation about their business or need — shows genuine attention and gets higher response rates.

Scoping calls: Ask about their goal, not just the task — "why do you need this" often reveals a better solution.

Setting expectations: Confirm scope, timeline, and revision rounds in writing before starting — prevents most freelance disputes.

Follow-up: A short check-in message partway through a project builds trust and catches misunderstandings early.
