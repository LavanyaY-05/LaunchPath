Schemes Path: AI Customer Support / Applied AI Startups

Best-fit schemes: SAMRIDH (MeitY) specifically targets software product startups during early acceleration and proof-of-concept. DPIIT recognition is the mandatory first step before applying to SAMRIDH or the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme.

Private schemes/funding fit: Applied AI/SaaS is one of the most actively funded categories by angel investors and early-stage VCs, since build cost is relatively low and iteration is fast.

What investors will check: A working demo, at least one pilot customer or clear signed interest, and a defensible narrow niche.
